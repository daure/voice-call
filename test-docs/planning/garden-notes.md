# Balcony Garden Notes

This is a fictional household gardening record.

## Plants

- Basil: east-facing windowsill, watered when the top layer of soil feels dry.
- Rosemary: balcony pot with drainage holes, sheltered from persistent rain.
- Mint: separate container because it spreads aggressively.

## Observations

On September 22, the basil leaned toward the morning light. Rotate the pot a quarter turn each week. A cracked saucer leaks onto the shelf; replace the saucer before increasing watering.

The yellow scarf belongs indoors and is not plant insulation. Sam has suggested naming the basil plant Lantern after Rowan Vale's poem, but Alex prefers the name Tram.

## Next visit to the garden shop

Buy one 18 cm saucer and a small bag of potting mix. Budget EUR 15. Measure the existing pot before buying a replacement container.
