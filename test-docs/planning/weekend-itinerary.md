# Delft Weekend Itinerary

This is a fictional travel plan, not a booking or a current opening-hours guide.

## Saturday

- 09:30: meet at the station and walk to the old market square.
- 10:15: coffee and pastries; allow EUR 18 for two people.
- 11:00: visit a pottery workshop; assumed cost EUR 24 per person.
- 13:00: lunch by the canal; budget EUR 35 for two.
- 15:00: look for the bookshop with the blue door mentioned in Sam's letter.
- 18:30: dinner; budget EUR 60 for two.

## Sunday

Take a short canal walk and buy a postcard before returning by train. If heavy rain is forecast, replace the canal walk with an indoor museum visit.

## Budget and constraints

The Saturday food and workshop budget is EUR 161 for two people. Set aside a further EUR 39 contingency, bringing the target to EUR 200 before transport and accommodation.

Check opening hours and accessibility before leaving. Neither workshop tickets nor dinner reservations have been made.
