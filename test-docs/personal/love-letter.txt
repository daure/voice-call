Dear Alex,

I still think about that rainy afternoon in Delft, when we missed the tram and found the little bookshop with the blue door. You bought a map of a city we had never visited. I bought a postcard I never sent, because everything I wanted to say was already sitting beside me.

You have a way of making ordinary things feel worth remembering: the first coffee of a slow Sunday, the crooked basil plant on our windowsill, the yellow scarf you insist is lucky.

I do not love you because every day is easy. I love the way we come back to each other after a difficult one. You listen before trying to solve anything. You make room for my unfinished thoughts.

For our anniversary, I would like to return to that bookshop, if it is still there. We can choose another impossible map and take the long route home.

With all my love,
Sam

This letter and its people are fictional.
