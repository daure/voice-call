# Autumn Reading List

These titles and authors are fictional.

| Title | Author | Genre | Discussion topic |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| The Lantern Keeper | Rowan Vale | Poetry | Quiet care and belonging |
| Maps for the Rain | Jules North | Travel essays | Unexpected journeys |
| The Reservation | Mina Brooks | Mystery | Conflicting promises |
| Four Windows | Ezra Stone | Short stories | Different views of one event |

The book club meets on the first Thursday of each month at 19:00. The October selection is Maps for the Rain. Bring one passage about a place you would like to visit.

Harbor Books offers a fictional book-club discount of ten percent on these four titles. Discount eligibility requires an active club membership; it does not apply to unrelated stock.
