# Business Use Case: Harbor Books Reservation Desk

All organizations, people, and figures in this document are fictional.

## Problem

Harbor Books receives 120 book reservation requests each week. Staff copy requests from email into a spreadsheet and spend about six minutes checking stock and replying to each customer. Duplicate requests can cause two staff members to reserve the same copy.

## Proposed service

A shared reservation desk records the requested title, customer reference, pickup deadline, and stock status. A staff member confirms each reservation before a customer receives a message. Only one active reservation can hold a particular physical copy.

## Financial assumptions

The service saves four minutes per request: eight staff hours per week. At an assumed labor cost of EUR 25 per hour, that is EUR 200 per week, or EUR 10,400 per 52-week year. These are freed staff hours, not guaranteed cash savings.

The setup budget is EUR 3,000. Annual software and support cost is EUR 1,200. First-year net capacity value is EUR 6,200 if the assumed savings hold.

## Pilot and decision

Run a four-week pilot with 20 requests per week. Owner: Maya Chen, store manager. Success requires at least three minutes saved per request and zero double reservations. Do not expand if either threshold fails.

The privacy risk is storing customer contact details. Keep contact details in the approved customer system and use references in the reservation desk.
